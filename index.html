Connect your shift feed

This board reads shifts from a Google Sheet that your Connecteam Zaps write to. Publish that sheet as CSV and paste the link below.

  1. In the Sheet: File → Share → Publish to web.
  2. Pick the tab your Zap writes to, choose Comma-separated values (.csv), and Publish.
  3. Copy the link (it looks like …/pub?gid=0&single=true&output=csv) and paste it here.

Publishing makes the sheet readable by anyone with the link. Keep only the columns the board needs (below) on the published tab.

Expected columns (header row, any order): shift_id, engineer, job_title, start, end, location, notes. Only engineer and job_title are required; shift_id is strongly recommended so updated shifts replace the old card instead of duplicating.

Settings

Set this to make card deletes also clear the row from the sheet, via a Zapier Catch Hook. Leave blank and deletes just hide the card for you.

A second published-CSV link — the sheet tab your erroring Zap writes to. Its rows appear under the Errors tab as cards you can action.

Status flags and hidden jobs are stored on this board for you only. Shift content always comes live from the sheet.
